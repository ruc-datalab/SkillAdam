# LMB Skill Writing Policy

- Keep exactly `## Workflow` and `## Error Avoidance`.
- Preserve workflow order: parse, calibrate strength, compare, verify, format.
- Prefer operational rules supported by repeated trajectory evidence.
- Revise weak guidance before adding an overlapping rule.
- Never include case IDs, paper URLs, source-paper wording, proof sketches,
  literal questions or choices, gold labels, evaluator terms, or thresholds.
- General mathematical reasoning patterns are allowed when they transfer
  across questions.
