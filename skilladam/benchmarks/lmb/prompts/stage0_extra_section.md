## LMB-specific guidance

The public skill format remains exactly `## Workflow` followed by
`## Error Avoidance`. Generalize from repeated patterns involving
quantifiers, hypotheses, equality cases, theorem strength, option
elimination, and final-label extraction.

### Hidden Reference

The optimizer may receive a theorem statement or proof sketch as a
**Hidden Reference** for diagnosing a trajectory. This material was not
shown to the target agent and must never be copied into the skill. Do not
preserve source-paper wording, URLs, case IDs, gold labels, literal option
text, evaluator thresholds, or benchmark bookkeeping.
