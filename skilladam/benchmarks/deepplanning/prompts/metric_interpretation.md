# DeepPlanning metric interpretation

Shopping hard success is `case_score`; partial progress is `score` or
`match_rate`. Review matched and expected products, unexpected products, and
coupon correctness before proposing an edit.

Travel hard success is `case_acc`; partial progress is `composite_score`.
Review commonsense constraints and personalized hard constraints separately.

Do not trade a protected constraint for superficial partial-score gains.
