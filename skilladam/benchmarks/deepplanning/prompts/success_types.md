# DeepPlanning success types

- **constraint_satisfaction**: All explicit hard constraints are verified.
- **efficient_tool_use**: Tool calls gather the necessary evidence without avoidable repetition.
- **complete_plan**: The final cart or itinerary includes every required component.
- **consistent_state_tracking**: Intermediate evidence remains consistent through finalization.
- **robust_final_verification**: A final check catches omissions and unexpected additions.
