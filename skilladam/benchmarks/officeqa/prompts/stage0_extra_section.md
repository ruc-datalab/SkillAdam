## OfficeQA-specific guidance

The public skill format remains exactly `## Workflow` followed by
`## Error Avoidance`. Generalize from repeated document-navigation,
table-alignment, operand-selection, arithmetic, and answer-format patterns.

Do not preserve case IDs, literal local paths, bulletin issue dates, source
URLs, one-off values, gold answers, evaluator thresholds, or benchmark
bookkeeping in the skill.
